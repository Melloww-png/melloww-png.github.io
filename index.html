I’m Daniel, a cybersecurity student who keeps asking, “How does that actually work?”

That curiosity turns into Python projects, security labs and plenty of trial and error. I enjoy tracing an alert back to its events, figuring out why a request slipped through and writing down what the evidence actually shows.

I’m studying cybersecurity at Coventry University through PSB Academy and preparing for TryHackMe SAL1. My focus is SOC analysis and cloud security, with patience, attention to detail and clear explanations at the centre of how I work.
